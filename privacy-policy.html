Privacy Policy – Nocturne Alarm

App: Nocturne Alarm · Package: com.aistudio.nocturnealarm.nxqvpm
Effective date: November 20, 2026 · Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Nocturne Alarm app ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. Nocturne Alarm is an alarm clock, timer, stopwatch and bedside clock for Android phones and tablets, Android TV, Amazon Fire tablets and Amazon Fire TV. It does not require an account.

1. Summary

  • Amazon Appstore edition (Fire tablets and Fire TV): the App collects no personal information. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDKs, does not use any advertising identifier, and does not request internet access. Nothing you enter in the App ever leaves your device.
  • Google Play edition: the App shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect limited device information to serve ads, as described in section 4. We (the developer) do not receive or store that information.

2. Information stored only on your device

The following data is created by you and stored locally on your device, in the App's private storage. We cannot access it, and it is never uploaded to us or anyone else:

  • Alarms: times, repeat days, labels, chosen chime, vibration, snooze and challenge settings.
  • Timer and stopwatch: durations, labels, saved presets and lap times.
  • Preferences: selected theme, 12/24-hour format and bedside display settings.
  • Custom chimes: if you choose an audio file from your device as a chime, the App stores only a reference to that one file (see section 3).

3. Custom audio files

When you pick your own audio file, the App uses the Android system file picker. The App is granted read access to the single file you select and nothing else. It does not request permission to read your photos, media library or other files, and the file is never uploaded.

4. Advertising (Google Play edition only)

The Amazon Appstore edition contains no ads.

The Google Play edition displays banner and occasional full-screen ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To serve, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location may be derived) and basic device and app-interaction information. This collection is performed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings.

5. Device permissions

PermissionWhy it is used
Exact alarms (USE_EXACT_ALARM / SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM)Ring alarms at the exact time you set.
Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED)Restore your alarms after the device restarts, the App updates, or the time zone changes.
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT)Show the ringing alarm over the lock screen with Snooze and Dismiss buttons.
Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK)Keep the alarm sound playing reliably while an alarm rings.

Internet, network state, advertising ID (Google Play edition only)Load ads from Google AdMob. Not present in the Amazon Appstore edition.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, trade or share personal information. Apart from Google AdMob in the Google Play edition (section 4), the App contains no third-party services.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep it in the App. You can delete individual alarms and presets in the App at any time, and you can delete all App data by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the App. Because we never receive your data, there is no copy held by us to delete.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information. Since the App does not send personal information to us, all such data is under your direct control on your device. For data collected by Google in the Google Play edition, you can exercise your rights with Google. You can contact us with any privacy question using the address below.

9. Children's privacy

The App is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. The Amazon Appstore edition collects no personal information from anyone, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Security

All App data is kept in the App's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
